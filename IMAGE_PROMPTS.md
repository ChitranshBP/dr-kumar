## General Guidelines for AI Image Generation:

1. **Style:** Professional medical/editorial photography or clean 3D renders
2. **Color Palette:** Each page has a primary brand color - use corresponding tones
   - Da Vinci: Blue/Cyan (#0e7490)
   - Self-Gripping Mesh: Teal (#14b8a6)
   - 3D Mesh: Amber/Gold (#f59e0b)
   - Biological Mesh: Emerald (#10b981)
   - ERAS Protocol: Purple/Violet (#a78bfa)
3. **Aspect Ratio:** 3:2 or 4:3 for hero images (600x400 suggested)
4. **Mood:** Professional, innovative, patient-focused, hopeful
5. **No People for Tech Pages:** Product/technology focused style preferred
6. **Include People for ERAS:** Lifestyle/recovery-focused imagery with actual people

---

## Image Specifications:
- **Hero image size:** 600x400 pixels, 3:2 aspect ratio
- **Format:** JPG/WebP preferred for web performance
- **Quality:** Minimum 80% quality, optimized for web
- **Alt text** should describe the medical concept clearly for accessibility
